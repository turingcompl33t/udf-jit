## Bibliography

This documents serves as an organization for references related to query execution, compilation, PL/SQL, UDFs, etc.

### Uncategorized

- Spark SQL: Relational Data Processing in Spark. Armbrust et al. 2015.
- Tupleware: Redefining Modern Analytics. Crotty et al. 2014.
- Orthogonal Optimization of Subqueries and Aggregation. Cesar A. Galindo-Legaria and Milin M. Joshi. 2001.
- Adaptive Optimizations of Recursive Queries in Teradata. Ghazal et al. 2012.

- Optimizing Cursor Loops in Relational Databases. Gupta et al. 2020.
