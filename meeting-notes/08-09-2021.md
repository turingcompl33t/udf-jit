## JIT-Compilation for User-Defined Functions

Meeting Notes

_August 9th, 2021_

### Notes

One thing I brought up during the meeting today was the overhead of query execution in the context of UDFs. For instance, if we are executing multiple queries in the body of a UDF, can we elide some of the overhead of setup and teardown for the query? As a stretch goal, would it be possible to optimize across execution domains (e.g. SQL query with UDF invocation -> UDF invocation with embedded SQL query -> SQL query)?

Andy brought up another potential "line of effort" for the proposal: context-sensitive optimization of UDF invocations. By "context-sensitive" we mean that certain optimizations will be beneficial in certain usage scenarios and less-so in others. Can we identify these situations and optimize accordingly? Professor Mowry brought up the possibility of compiling multiple versions of the UDF that might be more efficient in different situations, storing all of these, and then selecting the most-optimal function dynamically, once the calling query is encountered.

A final important topic of conversation was the structure of my MCDS capstone. For the capstone, I will pursue an evaluation similar to the one performed by Tanuj for his thesis work, but with the addition of a comparison with the Froid framework as well. Considering the engineering challenges we still face in executing certain query plans on NoisePage, this will not be trivial by any means...
